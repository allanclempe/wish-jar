Who can use Wish Jar

Wish Jar is for families. A parent or guardian sets up the family's jar and is responsible for how it's used, including by their kids. Kids can use Wish Jar with a parent's or guardian's permission.

Coins aren't money

Coins, bonus coins, consequences and wishes in Wish Jar are points the family keeps for itself. Wish Jar doesn't hold, move or pay out real money, and nothing can be bought in it. Whether and how a wish is turned into a real reward is up to each family.

Your family's jar

  • What you put in your jar is yours. You give Wish Jar permission to store it and show it on your family's devices, only so that Wish Jar works.
  • Only add photos you have the right to use, and keep join codes to yourself: anyone with a code can join your family while it lasts.
  • Each family has storage limits, and unused families are deleted automatically, as the Privacy Policy describes. Keep your own copy of anything you can't lose.

Using Wish Jar fairly

Don't use Wish Jar to store anything unlawful or harmful, don't try to reach another family's jar, and don't try to overload or get around the limits that keep Wish Jar running for everyone. Families that do may be removed.
